   b. Shadow evaluation: paired Arena evaluation Champion vs adaptation child (`submit_arena_job`), selection, invariants, Forge assessment — exactly the same calls evolve uses. The assessment is the canary's shadow evaluation.
   c. `canary start` with that assessment (through the existing `canary_transition_payload` path; do not reimplement), then for each stage submit a fresh paired evaluation Champion vs child, select, and call the existing advance policy with that evidence; a regression aborts (existing behavior) and the adaptation finishes with reason `CanaryAborted`; reaching 100% completes via the unchanged Promote policy and the adaptation finishes with reason `Promoted`. Respect freeze exactly as evolve/canary do (freeze halts advancement; never clears it) and the World's budget/cost Laws; bound the whole pipeline by a fixed documented paired-evaluation budget (e.g. 1 shadow + 4 stage evaluations = 5 trials).
   d. Every step records `drift.adaptation_*` events (started / finished with reason; intermediate steps are already durable as arena/selection/forge/canary events, so the finished event just cross-references them: proposal id, shadow evaluation id, assessment id, canary id, final canary stage, promotion transition if any). `verify_drift_adaptation_history` (in drift.rs or a new adaptation.rs) recomputes/cross-checks each event against the history before it, like `verify_evolution_history`, and fails closed on forged or out-of-order claims.
