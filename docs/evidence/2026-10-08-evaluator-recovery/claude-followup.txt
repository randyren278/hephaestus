## What "rejected" means

- `server.rs:1287-1292`: a `Rejected` error becomes `InvalidRequest`. The `rejected: Some(true)` flag is set only for explicit-admission commands, such as `EvaluatePairConfirmed`.
- Plain `EvaluatePair` leaves the flag out (`None`), and the test checks both cases.
- In practice, `rejected: true` means "refused at admission, nothing bound". It doesn't use up the evaluation ID: the same ID is admitted after a matching restart. That fits the protocol doc (`protocol.rs:2230`). You could add one sentence there saying a rejected ID stays reusable after you fix the cause.
